# MealMate AI

Build an app called "MealMate" - an AI meal planner that tells users exactly what to eat to hit their calorie and protein goals. The promise: "Eat this, get there." Users set their goal (cut/lean bulk/maintain/custom), calorie target, protein target, and pick a nearby grocery store. The app checks what's in stock at that store and generates a full day of meals using prepackaged foods with nutrition labels so there is zero guessing. Every item has a label - what the label says is what it is. Features: 1) Onboarding - goal selection, biometrics with auto TDEE calculation, store location selector, budget, food preferences/allergies. 2) Dashboard showing breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, drinks with calorie/protein totals. 3) Swap system for meals and individual ingredients. 4) Auto-generated shopping list organized by store aisle with prices. 5) Budget tracking per day. 6) Accountability check-in at end of day - "You said X, here's what happened." 7) Favorites and AI taste learning over time. 8) Drinks built into every plan - water, coffee, tea, protein shakes (Premier Protein, Fairlife). Use Supabase for auth and database. Mobile-first design, fresh greens and warm oranges color palette.
